# ADR [NNN]: [Short Decision Title]

> **What this document is:** an Architecture Decision Record. One small file per
> significant decision, so the reasoning survives and doesn't get re-litigated.
> You already make these inside session logs -- this pulls the important ones out
> where an agent or a new person finds them fast. Delete these prompt lines.

**Status:** PROPOSED | ACCEPTED | SUPERSEDED by ADR-[NNN]
**Date:** [DATE]
**Deciders:** [who]

## Context
> The situation forcing a decision. What's true, what's constrained, what's at
> stake. Enough that a stranger understands WHY a choice was even needed.

## Decision
> What was decided. One clear statement.

## Alternatives considered
> The other real options, each with its trade-off. This is the most valuable
> part -- it shows the decision wasn't made blind, and it's what you re-read when
> someone later asks "why didn't we just...".

## Consequences
> What this decision makes easy, and what it makes hard or forecloses. Both. An
> honest ADR names the cost it accepted, not just the benefit it bought.
